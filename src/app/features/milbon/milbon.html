Signature Haircare

Milbon's 360° Beautiful Hair concept is a comprehensive hair care approach designed to improve the hair's condition from every angle. This system targets structural damage at a microscopic level, specifically addressing "SSVs" (stick-shaped voids) that form within the hair and lead to dryness, breakage, and loss of shine.

Using Milbon's patented SSVR-Silk™ technology, the system works to fill these internal gaps, restoring strength, smoothness, and overall hair integrity. The result is hair that is more resilient, balanced, and naturally healthy-looking over time.

This collection is designed to support both in-salon treatments and at-home maintenance, creating a continuous care cycle that maintains results between visits.

Gold Haircare

Milbon's Luxury Line represents the highest level of Japanese haircare, combining over 60 years of research with advanced formulation technology. Designed for clients seeking elevated results, this collection focuses on restoring hair at both a structural and surface level using proprietary repair systems and high-performance ingredients.

Formulated with SSVR-Silk™ and SSVR-Collagen, the system targets early-stage damage within the hair fiber, helping to reinforce strength, improve elasticity, and enhance overall hair integrity. The result is hair that feels smoother, stronger, and visibly more refined over time.

The Luxury Line is divided into targeted solutions: Indulging Hydration, Illuminating Glow, Vitalizing Dimension, and Enhancing Vivacity, each designed to address specific needs such as hydration, shine, density, and scalp health, while maintaining a consistent, high-end sensory experience.

Monochromatic

Milbon's Monochromatic collection is a modern styling system designed for versatility, buildability, and ease of use. Developed in collaboration with a global creative director, this line focuses on simplifying the styling process while delivering consistent, salon-level results.

The collection is built around lightweight, multi-functional formulas that can be layered to create volume, texture, definition, or movement without weighing the hair down. Each product is designed to work individually or together, allowing for a customizable approach to styling based on the desired finish.

Monochromatic supports effortless styling by enhancing the hair's natural behavior, providing heat protection, frizz control, and flexible hold — while maintaining a soft, natural look. The result is controlled, dynamic hair that moves in harmony with the individual's lifestyle.

Pro Products

Milbon's Pro Products collection is designed to support in-salon technical services with a complete system of professional-use formulas. This category includes color, developers, treatments, and supporting products engineered for precision, consistency, and controlled performance during application.

Each product is developed to integrate seamlessly into professional workflows, allowing stylists to execute services such as coloring, straightening, perming, and repair treatments with accuracy and reliability. The formulations prioritize both technical results and hair integrity, ensuring performance without compromising long-term condition.

The Pro Products system reflects Milbon's commitment to combining advanced research with practical salon use, providing stylists with the tools needed to deliver consistent, high-level results across all services.
